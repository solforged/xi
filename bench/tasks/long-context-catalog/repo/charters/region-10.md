# Service charter for region-10

This charter is the original signed schedule. Read the matching revision memorandum before implementing the active schedule. All sixteen named operations have independent contracts. A number in prose is exact, not an example. Durations here are seconds.

## archive

The admission desk accepts thirty-eight units during one accounting window; this is the quota, not the burst allowance. Short surges have a separate allowance of twenty-eight units, stored as burst without adding it to quota. The window lasts eighty-four seconds; a fresh accounting window begins only after that duration. Cached results remain usable for one hundred and ninety-five seconds, the ttl, even when the accounting window is shorter. Delivery gets seven total attempts including the first one; do not interpret this number as additional retries. Between failed attempts the initial backoff is ten seconds. A delayed acknowledgement has a grace period of twelve seconds, distinct from both backoff and ttl. Every accepted unit leaves a complete audit record; sampled or absent audit records are not compliant. Queue order follows declared preference classes before arrival time; the normalized order is priority. Exhausted capacity silently discards the newest unit, so overflow is drop. The indivisible accounting unit is a request; store that singular word as unit. The operation is enabled for ordinary traffic; its normalized enabled flag is true.
