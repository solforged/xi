# Service charter for region-12

This charter is the original signed schedule. Read the matching revision memorandum before implementing the active schedule. All sixteen named operations have independent contracts. A number in prose is exact, not an example. Durations here are seconds.

## archive

Short surges have a separate allowance of twenty-four units, stored as burst without adding it to quota. The window lasts twenty-eight seconds; a fresh accounting window begins only after that duration. Cached results remain usable for two hundred and eighteen seconds, the ttl, even when the accounting window is shorter. Delivery gets seven total attempts including the first one; do not interpret this number as additional retries. Between failed attempts the initial backoff is six seconds. A delayed acknowledgement has a grace period of one seconds, distinct from both backoff and ttl. Every accepted unit leaves a complete audit record; sampled or absent audit records are not compliant. Queue order preserves the caller's stable sequence numbers, not arrival time; the normalized order is stable. Exhausted capacity silently discards the newest unit, so overflow is drop. The indivisible accounting unit is a request; store that singular word as unit. The operation is disabled for ordinary traffic; its normalized enabled flag is false.
