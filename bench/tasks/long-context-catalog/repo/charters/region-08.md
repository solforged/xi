# Service charter for region-08

This charter is the original signed schedule. Read the matching revision memorandum before implementing the active schedule. All sixteen named operations have independent contracts. A number in prose is exact, not an example. Durations here are seconds.

## archive

The admission desk accepts one hundred and twenty-two units during one accounting window; this is the quota, not the burst allowance. Short surges have a separate allowance of ten units, stored as burst without adding it to quota. The window lasts forty-one seconds; a fresh accounting window begins only after that duration. Cached results remain usable for three hundred and twenty-five seconds, the ttl, even when the accounting window is shorter. Delivery gets four total attempts including the first one; do not interpret this number as additional retries. Between failed attempts the initial backoff is eight seconds. A delayed acknowledgement has a grace period of ten seconds, distinct from both backoff and ttl. No audit record is retained for this operation; neither sampling nor a complete audit is authorized. Queue order preserves the caller's stable sequence numbers, not arrival time; the normalized order is stable. Exhausted capacity refuses the new unit immediately, so overflow is reject. The indivisible accounting unit is a request; store that singular word as unit. The operation is enabled for ordinary traffic; its normalized enabled flag is true.
