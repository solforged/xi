# Service charter for region-06

This charter is the original signed schedule. Read the matching revision memorandum before implementing the active schedule. All sixteen named operations have independent contracts. A number in prose is exact, not an example. Durations here are seconds.

## archive

The admission desk accepts one hundred and fifty-five units during one accounting window; this is the quota, not the burst allowance. Short surges have a separate allowance of twenty-nine units, stored as burst without adding it to quota. The window lasts forty-six seconds; a fresh accounting window begins only after that duration. Cached results remain usable for three hundred and fifty seconds, the ttl, even when the accounting window is shorter. Delivery gets three total attempts including the first one; do not interpret this number as additional retries. Between failed attempts the initial backoff is nine seconds. A delayed acknowledgement has a grace period of two seconds, distinct from both backoff and ttl. Only sampled audit records are retained; the charter deliberately does not require a full record for each accepted unit. Queue order follows declared preference classes before arrival time; the normalized order is priority. Exhausted capacity keeps the new unit for a later window, so overflow is defer. The indivisible accounting unit is a record; store that singular word as unit. The operation is disabled for ordinary traffic; its normalized enabled flag is false.
