This charter is the original signed schedule. Read the matching revision memorandum before implementing the active schedule. All sixteen named operations have independent contracts. A number in prose is exact, not an example. Durations here are seconds.

## archive

Short surges have a separate allowance of eleven units, stored as burst without adding it to quota. The window lasts twenty-eight seconds; a fresh accounting window begins only after that duration. Cached results remain usable for two hundred and ten seconds, the ttl, even when the accounting window is shorter. Delivery gets two total attempts including the first one; do not interpret this number as additional retries. Between failed attempts the initial backoff is seven seconds. A delayed acknowledgement has a grace period of eleven seconds, distinct from both backoff and ttl.
