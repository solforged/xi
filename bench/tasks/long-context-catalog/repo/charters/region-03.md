# Service charter for region-03

This charter is the original signed schedule. Read the matching revision memorandum before implementing the active schedule. All sixteen named operations have independent contracts. A number in prose is exact, not an example. Durations here are seconds.

## archive

The admission desk accepts one hundred and thirty-nine units during one accounting window; this is the quota, not the burst allowance. Short surges have a separate allowance of twenty-one units, stored as burst without adding it to quota. The window lasts fifty-two seconds; a fresh accounting window begins only after that duration. Cached results remain usable for four hundred and thirty-two seconds, the ttl, even when the accounting window is shorter. Delivery gets two total attempts including the first one; do not interpret this number as additional retries. Between failed attempts the initial backoff is five seconds. A delayed acknowledgement has a grace period of fourteen seconds, distinct from both backoff and ttl. Every accepted unit leaves a complete audit record; sampled or absent audit records are not compliant. Queue order preserves the caller's stable sequence numbers, not arrival time; the normalized order is stable. Exhausted capacity keeps the new unit for a later window, so overflow is defer. The indivisible accounting unit is a request; store that singular word as unit. The operation is enabled for ordinary traffic; its normalized enabled flag is true.
